Consumer Duty framing: The FCA's Consumer Duty (PS22/9) requires firms to deliver good outcomes across four areas: products & services, price & value, consumer understanding, and consumer support. Complaint uphold rates are a primary FCA supervisory metric. A sustained uphold rate above 50% in any product area indicates systematic failure to deliver the Duty — not merely a complaint-handling issue.
